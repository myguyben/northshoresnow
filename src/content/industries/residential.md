## Winter, off your to-do list

You don't book us storm by storm. Once your home is set up, service is **automatic**: our
dispatch watches the forecast and local conditions, crews roll when snowfall hits the
trigger, and de-icing happens on the freezing mornings in between. After every visit you
get photo confirmation of the finished work — so even if you're away, you know it's done.

**Quoted from satellite, not a sales visit.** Send us your address and we map your
driveway, walkways, steps and sidewalk frontage from satellite imagery, then price the
work from the measured area. Most homes get a quote the same day, with a map showing
exactly what's included.

**Priced the way you prefer.** Pay per visit, or take a seasonal plan that turns winter
into one fixed monthly amount from October to April — same price whether it snows twice
or twenty times. Our guide to
[seasonal vs per-event contracts](/blog/seasonal-vs-per-event-snow-removal-contracts)
explains the trade-off.

## Built for North Shore homes

The [North Shore](/north-shore) is the snowiest place people commute from in Metro
Vancouver — [Lynn Valley](/lynn-valley), [Edgemont](/edgemont-village),
[Seymour](/seymour) and the [British Properties](/west-vancouver) all sit hundreds of
metres up the mountainside. Steep driveways, bylaw sidewalk deadlines and freeze-thaw ice
are exactly the conditions our commercial crews have handled for years — the same
equipment and the same weather triggers now serve houses across
[North Vancouver](/north-vancouver) and [West Vancouver](/west-vancouver).

One practical note: if your driveway concrete was poured within the last year, tell us —
fresh concrete shouldn't be de-iced, and we'll plan around it.

## Simple paperwork, easy exit

Residential clients get a short homeowner agreement, not a commercial contract: the
mapped service area, your rates, the season dates, and a 30-day cancellation clause.
Salting follows the same triggers we use on commercial sites — temperature and surface
readings, documented — so your steps are treated before the school run, not after the
slip. Get a quote from our [contact page](/contact) or call (604) 990-7072.
